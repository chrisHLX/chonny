# Forever Observer: where things stand, and the next step (2026-11-10)

A handover from the session on Chriso's desktop to whichever session picks this up next (first, the
one on the laptop). Read this, then `tools/wow-addon/MindCollectorObserver/README.md`, before changing
the addon, the /ask bridge or the market scripts.

## What exists

- **The addon** (`tools/wow-addon/MindCollectorObserver/`) records the items you look at, auction house
  scans, prices, your professions, the trainers and vendors you open, and a ledger of crafting, buying
  and selling. Its window has these tabs: Items, Professions, Market, Ledger, Ask. Professions shows
  cost and profit per skill point and where each recipe comes from.
- **Recipe data** (`Data.lua`) is generated by `tools/forever-market/observer_data.py` from the client's
  DB2 tables (wago.tools product `wowf`), with Wowhead's Forever facts laid over it from
  `tools/forever-market/wowhead/*.json`. Only `alchemy.json` exists so far.
- **/ask** (`tools/forever-ask/`): a question typed in game is saved at a reload. `ask_bridge.py`
  answers it with headless Claude, using the web, Wowhead in the player's Chrome and item facts from
  the game files, and writes the answer to `Answers.lua`. Start the bridge with
  `start-ask-bridge.cmd`; closing its window stops it.
- **The desktop app** has the observer and Ask built in (0.6.11, in the repo). **It's not released or
  installed.** Chriso said not yet, because it might break video recording. Don't install or release it
  without asking.
- **Read-aloud** for Claude Code replies: `tools/claude-speak/README.md`.

closed**, because WoW overwrites the file when you log out. The file is the plain Lua table
`MindCollectorObserverDB`. These keys hold most of it:

| Key | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `lastScan`, `prevScan` | The last two full scans: `{t = time, items = {[itemID] = {unit, count, name}}}` |
| `prices` | One reading per item per day, for every item you looked at or scanned |
| `tracked`, `track` | Which items you're tracking, and every reading of them (`track` appears once a tracked item gets a new reading; not in this upload yet) |
| `ledger` | Crafts, AH buys and sales, vendor buys and sales, posts. Sales come from mail invoices, so they carry buyer names |
| `trainers`, `trainerSeen`, `vendors`, `vendorPrices` | What each trainer or vendor you opened teaches or sells, and where it stands |
| `asks` | /ask questions, with character, faction, zone and position |

## Shared auction prices: built 2026-11-12, not yet deployed or released

Chriso's direction (2026-11-10, 11-12): everyone's auction house scans on the server, so everyone
with the app gets them. Then price history and "is it actually selling" stop depending on one player
scanning. Sharing is **on by default with a clear switch** (Chriso's call, 2026-11-12).

How it works:

1. **The addon** records each scan's realm, region and faction on `lastScan` (`main.lua` finishScan).
   Scans saved before this have none and are never sent.
2. **The app** (`tools/desktop-app/MarketShare.cs`, own timer, every failure logged) watches the
   addon's saved file. Each scan newer than the last one sent goes to `POST /api/forever/scan` as
   gzipped JSON: per item the cheapest unit price, how many are listed and how many sit in each
   time-left band. Nothing else in the file is read for it. Then `GET /api/forever/market` (after a
   send, else every 30 minutes) is written into the addon as `Market.lua` for the next /reload.
   State is in `market.json` beside settings.json. Settings: "Share my auction house scans"
   (`ShareScans`); prices come back either way.
3. **The server** (`ForeverMarketService`, `ForeverMarketController`, tables `forever_scans` and
   `forever_prices`): a market is `REGION/Realm/Faction`. Each scan is kept whole, gzipped, in
   `storage/app/forever/scans/`, and merged into one reading per market, item and hour: the most
   complete scan wins, a later one breaks a tie, `sources` counts them. Refused: scans over 14 days
   old or from the future, scans more than 3x the market's biggest of the last 3 days, mostly
   unreadable items, and over 12 an hour per player. Readings older than 30 days are dropped (the
   scans stay). The pooled view is cached until the market's next scan. `ForeverMarketTest`.
4. **The addon reads the pool**: `latestPrice` takes a pooled reading when it's newer than the
   player's own, the "selling" read uses the pooled pair when newer (marked "(all players)"), and
   the footer says how many players' scans the prices come from.

Checked 2026-11-12 with `MindCollector.exe --share-market` against a local site: Chriso's real scan
of 11 Nov (194,825 auctions, 5,147 items, all with time-left bands) was sent, stored, pooled and
written back as 388 KB of Lua that parses; item 15347 matched the saved scan exactly. The same scan
showed Forever counts the time-left bands from 1 and caps the longest at 24 hours.

Still open:

- **Markets, settled 2026-11-12:** Forever is one realm per region (Chriso's WTF holds one realm
  folder, `91`, for all five characters, whose names carry suffixes like Crypto-Shot; portal US),
  and Horde and Alliance don't share an auction house (Chriso). So NA is two markets, one per
  faction, which is what the `REGION/Realm/Faction` key already gives. **Plus a third: the neutral
  goblin auction house** (Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Everlook) has its own listings (Chriso). The addon
  tells which house is open when it opens (`houseFaction()`: those towns, or an auctioneer of

  at the Azeroth Commerce Authority camp outside the Crossroads (Three Corners for Alliance), whose
  spot rotates between a bank, a mailbox and him: Wowhead's ACA guide calls his a neutral house,

  against the Horde house's 205,329. Each scan records `where` since 0.6.15), notes no browse prices there, keeps
  its scan in `DB.otherScans.Neutral` instead of `lastScan`, and the app sends it as faction
  `Neutral`, its own market. The pool the app brings back is always the player's own faction's
  (`side`). Not yet seen in game: whether a goblin auctioneer reports no faction; the town names
  cover the three known houses either way.
- **Scale.** `pooled()` reads up to 7 days of hourly readings per market in one pass; fine at a few
  players, worth a daily summary table if a market gets many.
- **To ship it:** deploy (migrations run in `deploy.sh`), then build and publish the app (0.6.11 is
  still unreleased; it carries this, the Observer and /ask). The addon's new `Market.lua` needs a

## Ask without Claude Code (2026-11-13, app 0.6.22)

- **Who answers.** By default the website: `POST /api/forever/ask` (`ForeverAskController`,
  `ForeverAskService`) asks Gemini 2.5 Flash with Google Search grounding for a JSON answer of at most
  two sentences and points. Claude Code answers instead when Settings has it ticked and it's installed
  (`AskBridge.UseClaude`). `AppSettings.AskOn` (default on) is answering at all; `AskClaude` now means
  "use Claude instead".
- **Coordinates.** Google Search sees page text, not Wowhead's map data, and Wowhead answers this
  server with 403 (tested 13 Nov), so a pin is kept only when a page wrote the coordinates out; Gemini
  is told never to estimate one. The first real answer (Recipe: Frost Oil) gave Bro'kin in Alterac
  Mountains, 38.2, 38.9, from metabot.gg, fandom.com and foreveroth.com: Classic-era pages, so the
  in-game answer names its sources. Claude stays the exact option.
- **Cost and limits.** Each answer is kept 3 days by question and faction, so a repeat by anyone costs
  nothing (0.4s against 7.8s). 30 fresh answers a player a day; logged as `AiRequest` purpose
  `forever_ask`. Gemini 2.5 Flash search grounding: about 1,500 free a day, then about $35 per 1,000
  (Google's pricing, checked 13 Nov; it was changing for Gemini 3).
- **Two bugs found on the way (0.6.20, 0.6.21).** The saved-data reader's regexes searched the rest of
  the file on a miss (57s for 1 MB, the app froze), and its number `?:` was typed double, so
  `t is long` never matched and every question read as asked in 1970 and was skipped silently. A
  question while answering is off now gets an answer saying so.

## Prices by time of day (2026-11-14, app 0.6.25)

Chriso's question of 12 Nov: are prices higher in Aussie evening hours than at US peak? `ui.lua`
answers it in two places from `DB.history` (every full scan kept, 0.6.15, `keepScan`):

- **An item's page** (Tracked, Market): the median cheapest price and listings in each four-hour block
  of the player's clock that has readings, dearest and cheapest marked.
- **The Market tab's panel before anything is picked**: the whole house by block. Each scan's level is
  the median over its items of price / that item's median price across every scan kept; a block is
  the mean of its scans. Usual, not a fixed day, so a joke listing can't move it. With two scans the
  halves are symmetric by construction and the panel says so until six.

Checked on 14 Nov against the real saved file (two scans, 5,221 items in both) through
`tod_test.py` in the scratchpad and Redis's Lua: 96 ms, Thursday 12:00-16:00 1.8% above, 00:00-04:00
1.8% below. The whole-house walk is cached by scan count, so it runs once per new scan.

## Traps already hit

- **Never toggle trainer filters while reading a trainer** (`SetTrainerServiceTypeFilter`). Each
  toggle fires `TRAINER_UPDATE`, and the loop froze the game.
- SavedVariables are written only at a /reload or logout. A new file in the addon needs a full game
  restart, not a /reload.
- **Wowhead rate-limits after about 40 fast page loads** (HTTP 405). Fetch slowly, one page every few
  seconds, in the player's own Chrome. No bot-check workarounds.
- **Still to fetch from Wowhead:** 99 of 137 Alchemy recipe items' vendors and drops, and all of First
  Aid and Cooking. Ask Chriso before a long crawl.
- **Quests for crafting items are done** (2026-11-12, `wowhead/quests.json`): the 258 crafting items
  Wowhead lists as quest objectives (filter 85), 504 quests. Even at one page every 5 seconds Wowhead
  sent about 20 HTTP 405s; a 60-second pause after each one got through with nothing lost. The data
  came out of Chrome in 400-character pieces and was checked against the page's length and hash.
  The client has no quest-objective table, so Wowhead is the only source for this.
- **WoW's `string.format("%d")` stops at 2^31** (2026-11-13). A listing at 9,999,999 gold is
  999,999,900,000 copper; `%d` on it throws, WoW hides addon errors, and because the price history was
  written before the scan was kept, every scan after 0.6.15 was thrown away in silence. `%.0f` for
  anything in copper or a percentage of a price move. `keepScan` runs under `pcall` now, and says so in
  chat if it fails.
- **Redraw on an event, not in it** (2026-11-14). `ns.refresh` is asked for by every look, price, scan
  tick and ledger line; the window used to redraw at each, and on the Market tab each redraw walked
  all ~11,000 scanned items with a name lookup each. Now `refresh` gathers the asks into one `redraw`
  50 ms later, nothing runs while the window is hidden, and the Market walk is cached until a scan, a
  search or an item name changes. Keep it that way: new code calls `ns.refresh()`, never draws itself.
- **Checking the addon compiles without WoW**: there's no Lua on this PC, but Herd's Redis is Lua 5.1,
  the same as WoW. Wrap the file in `local f = function(...) <file> end return 1` (the scratchpad's
  `luawrap.py` did it) and `redis-cli --eval wrapped.lua`: `1` means it compiled, "Error compiling
  script" with a line number means it didn't. Do it before copying a file into the game.
- Check Forever's UI APIs against Gethe/wow-ui-source, branch `forever`; don't guess.
