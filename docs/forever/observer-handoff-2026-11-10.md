# Forever Observer: where things stand, and the next step (2026-11-10)

A handover from the session on Chriso's desktop to whichever session picks this up next (first, the
one on the laptop). Read this, then `tools/wow-addon/MindCollectorObserver/README.md`, before changing
the addon, the /ask bridge or the market scripts.

**Named MindCollector Auction House since 2026-11-22** (Chriso). Only the name shown changed: the
window title, the minimap button, the AddOns list, the app and the site. The folder, the `.toc` file,
`MindCollectorObserverDB`, the SavedVariables file and `/mco` keep "Observer", because renaming the
folder would orphan every player's saved scans, ledger and looks. Code and older docs still say Observer.

## What exists

- **The addon** (`tools/wow-addon/MindCollectorObserver/`) records the items you look at, auction house
  scans, prices, your professions, the trainers and vendors you open, and a ledger of crafting, buying
  and selling. Its window has these tabs: Items, Professions, Market, Ledger, Ask. Professions shows
  cost and profit per skill point and where each recipe comes from.
- **Recipe data** (`Data.lua`) is generated by `tools/forever-market/observer_data.py` from the client's
  DB2 tables (wago.tools product `wowf`), with Wowhead's Forever facts laid over it from
  `tools/forever-market/wowhead/*.json`. Only `alchemy.json` exists so far.
- **/ask** (`tools/forever-ask/`): a question typed in game is saved at a reload. `ask_bridge.py`
  answers it with headless Claude, using the web, Wowhead in the player's Chrome and item facts from
  the game files, and writes the answer to `Answers.lua`. Start the bridge with
  `start-ask-bridge.cmd`; closing its window stops it.
- **The desktop app** has the observer and Ask built in (0.6.11, in the repo). **It's not released or
  installed.** Chriso said not yet, because it might break video recording. Don't install or release it
  without asking.
- **Read-aloud** for Claude Code replies: `tools/claude-speak/README.md`.

## Shared auction prices: built 2026-11-12, not yet deployed or released

Chriso's direction (2026-11-10, 11-12): everyone's auction house scans on the server, so everyone
with the app gets them. Then price history and "is it actually selling" stop depending on one player
scanning. Sharing is **on by default with a clear switch** (Chriso's call, 2026-11-12).

How it works:

1. **The addon** records each scan's realm, region and faction on `lastScan` (`main.lua` finishScan).
   Scans saved before this have none and are never sent.
2. **The app** (`tools/desktop-app/MarketShare.cs`, own timer, every failure logged) watches the
   addon's saved file. Each scan newer than the last one sent goes to `POST /api/forever/scan` as
   gzipped JSON: per item the cheapest unit price, how many are listed and how many sit in each
   time-left band. Nothing else in the file is read for it. Then `GET /api/forever/market` (after a
   send, else every 30 minutes) is written into the addon as `Market.lua` for the next /reload.
   State is in `market.json` beside settings.json. Settings: "Share my auction house scans"
   (`ShareScans`); prices come back either way.
3. **The server** (`ForeverMarketService`, `ForeverMarketController`, tables `forever_scans` and
   `forever_prices`): a market is `REGION/Realm/Faction`. Each scan is kept whole, gzipped, in
   `storage/app/forever/scans/`, and merged into one reading per market, item and hour: the most
   complete scan wins, a later one breaks a tie, `sources` counts them. Refused: scans over 14 days
   old or from the future, scans more than 3x the market's biggest of the last 3 days, mostly
   unreadable items, and over 12 an hour per player. Readings older than 30 days are dropped (the
   scans stay). The pooled view is cached until the market's next scan. `ForeverMarketTest`.
4. **The addon reads the pool**: `latestPrice` takes a pooled reading when it's newer than the
   player's own, the "selling" read uses the pooled pair when newer (marked "(all players)"), and
   the footer says how many players' scans the prices come from.

Checked 2026-11-12 with `MindCollector.exe --share-market` against a local site: Chriso's real scan
of 11 Nov (194,825 auctions, 5,147 items, all with time-left bands) was sent, stored, pooled and
written back as 388 KB of Lua that parses; item 15347 matched the saved scan exactly. The same scan
showed Forever counts the time-left bands from 1 and caps the longest at 24 hours.

Still open:

## Crafting Profits on the site (2026-11-26)

Chriso: "the website could display the most profitable crafts for the week". `/wow/forever/crafts`
(`ForeverCraftsController`, `ForeverCraftsService`, `forever/crafts.blade.php`, `ForeverCraftsTest`).

- **Recipes** are the addon's own: `ForeverRecipes` reads `Data.lua` (ns.recipes, itemNames, vendor,
  and ns.wh laid over as ui.lua does), so the page and the Professions tab can't disagree. 1,965 of
  its 2,145 recipes make an item from materials. Re-generating Data.lua changes the page with it.
- **The sum** is ui.lua's `profitsFor`: the product's price less the 5% cut, times what a craft makes
  (Data.lua field 12, the client's own count: Fire Oil 2, Cured Rugged Hide 2, Thorium Grenade 3), less
  the materials at their price or the vendor's when lower. A price is the median cheapest listing over
  the week's readings, not the newest, and materials are bought, never made (the site doesn't know
  your professions).
- **The addon prices what you make at its usual price too (2026-11-26, app 0.6.38).** Chriso
  made Elixir of the Mongoose on the addon's "76s a craft", which came from one dear hour (the pool
  held 82s on 20 Nov and 1g 50s on the 26th, 171 listed against 1,155). The pool now carries two more
  fields per item, the 7-day median and how many hourly readings it is of, and the app passes them
  through unchanged (any app version). ui.lua's `usualPrice` takes whichever median has more readings,
  the pool's or your own scans', three at least; `sellValue` uses it, and every "sells" line reads
  "usually 83s, 1g 50s now (81% over)" when today's price is 10% or more away. Reagents stay at today's
  price, which is what buying them costs. With no usual price yet the line says so and uses today's.
- **The week** is the 7 days up to the market's newest scan, so a market nobody scanned lately keeps
  its last week, and the page says when it was. Cached until the market's next scan (~1.3s to build
  locally on the 23-scan market).
- **Steady:** by default only products on the AH in at least half the week's readings. The first run
  was topped by gear with one listing (Lionheart Helm at 1,200g, seen in 5 of 23); "Include items
  rarely on the AH" shows them.
- **Cooldowns** from the game's SpellCooldowns (`forever:recipe-cooldowns {dir} --download` writes
  `data/forever/recipe-cooldowns.json`): 15 recipes, Mooncloth 95 hours, Transmute: Arcanite and
  Legionite 47 hours, nine transmutes 23 hours, all those shared as "Transmute - Alchemy".
- **First look** (US2 Horde, 13 to 20 Nov, 23 scans by one player): 1,219 crafts priced, 8 with a
  material never listed, 617 profitable among steady items.

## Gear: every weapon and armour piece from the game (2026-11-27, building)

Chriso: recommend the best gear a player can get at their level, from the AH and from drops; vendor
gear like the battleground reputation items too. Built: the addon side (`Gear.lua`, `GearIds.lua`),
the website's storage (`ForeverGearService`, `Api\ForeverGearController`, migration
`2026_11_27_000001_create_forever_gear`, `ForeverGearTest`), the app's sending (`MarketShare`, next
app version) and the page, the Gear Finder (`/wow/forever/gear`, `ForeverGearFinder`,
`ForeverGearFinderTest`; `docs/site-pages.md`). **The player picks the stats and their weights**
(Chriso, 2026-11-27: "we just filter gear and level"), so the site holds no stat priorities.

- **Why the addon.** wago's copy of Forever's ItemSparse holds stats for only 7,921 of the 19,048
  weapon and armour numbers in Item.csv; the server sends the rest when the game asks
  (`C_Item.RequestLoadItemDataByID`, `ITEM_DATA_LOAD_RESULT`), which is how loot addons fill boss
  pages (the installed ForeverDungeonJournal does the same; its loot lists are typed in from
  wowsrc.com and the like). A probe on 26 Nov: all ten items the files also describe came back with
  the files' stats; answers in ~0.01s, refusals in ~0.35s; 500 at 25 a second drew no complaint.
- **`/mco items`** asks for every number in `GearIds.lua` (`tools/forever-market/gear_ids.py` from
  wago Item.csv) at 25 a second, pausing in combat, carrying on after a /reload, once per build. It
  waits with `IsItemDataCachedByID`, never `GetItemInfo`, which asks the server again (the probe asked
  each refused item ~20 times that way). Chriso's first sweep (build 1.60.1.71139): **13,146 in the
  game, 5,902 refused, none unanswered.** Refused = not in Forever: other WoW versions' numbers, and
  raid gear (MC tier, ZG, AQ; no raid is out yet, Chriso).
- **What an item carries:** name, quality, item level, required level, slot, class/subclass, bind,
  sell price, `GetItemStats` (77 keys; ITEM_MOD_ and _SHORT dropped, RESISTANCE0_NAME is armour), weapon
  damage and speed from the tooltip, and the tooltip lines starting Equip:, Use:, Chance on hit:,
  Classes: (1,586 items), Unique, and Requires other than level (reputation such as "The Defilers -
  Revered", PvP rank such as "Legionnaire", professions).
- **Required level isn't availability.** Many bind-on-pickup drops have none: the "best Agility head
  a level 40 can wear" came out as Darkmantle Cap and Beastmaster's Cap, level-60 dungeon set pieces.
  A drop needs its source's level. There are also same-name items under two numbers (two Darkmantle
  Caps, 25 and 26 Agility): look before deduping.
- **Suffix versions.** Forever marks "of the Bandit" as a bonus number on the link (item:15602 with
  bonus 12751), not the old suffix field. Two layouts: a shift-clicked link has the count in field 12
  (`15602::::::::41:1485::1:12751`), an auction house link has a context field first and the count in
  13 (`1994::::::::1:1489::1:1:12727:1:28:5255`). 0.6.39 read only the first, so every auction's key
  was "1"; the server refuses one-digit bonuses and a migration dropped those rows. A scan keeps each
  version's cheapest price and count in the item's `v` (`GetReplicateItemLink`), the app sends them
  as the scan's `variants`, and `forever_variant_prices` merges them by the same rule as prices. Each
  new version's stats go to `DB.variants` after the scan. The files have the suffix tables too
  (ItemBonus type 2 is a stat's share of RandPropPoints, type 5 the name: 6.07, 15.6 and 13.81 at item
  level 45, Ancient Crown of the Bandit's 6, 16 and 14), a cross-check, not the source.
- **Vendors:** every list the player opens, by NPC number: price, other costs ([[count, item number or
  currency name]] from `GetMerchantItemCostItem`), stock, and Requires lines from the merchant
  tooltip. A newer list replaces the vendor's rows; an older one sent late changes nothing.
- **Sending:** a finished sweep once, in parts of 4,000 items (nginx takes 1 MB; the whole sweep is
  497 KB gzipped, the parts about 150 KB). `--gear-json FILE DIR` writes the parts without sending;
  checked against the sweep read straight from the file: all 13,146 items identical. A refusal never
  overwrites an item a sweep found. Anyone with an app key can send; the newest wins, `user_id` kept.
- **Not yet tried in game:** a scan's suffix versions and a vendor list (the sweep has been).
- **Testing it by hand:** the app replaces its own copy of the addon at every start, so the test
  copy in Chriso's AddOns folder has its `.installed-by-mindcollector` marker removed (the app then
  leaves it alone, logging "a different copy"). Put the marker back, or Settings > Install the addon,
  once the version carrying Gear.lua ships.

## Ask without Claude Code (2026-11-13, app 0.6.22; removed 2026-11-27, app 0.6.40)

**Removed.** Chriso, 2026-11-27: the built-in features cover what /ask was for, and the bridge and the

Answers.lua, the Research and "Ask where to get it" buttons, the app's AskBridge with its Ask tab and

record of how it worked.

- **Who answers.** By default the website: `POST /api/forever/ask` (`ForeverAskController`,

  two sentences and points. Claude Code answers instead when Settings has it ticked and it's installed
  (`AskBridge.UseClaude`). `AppSettings.AskOn` (default on) is answering at all; `AskClaude` now means
  "use Claude instead".
- **Coordinates.** Google Search sees page text, not Wowhead's map data, and Wowhead answers this
  server with 403 (tested 13 Nov), so a pin is kept only when a page wrote the coordinates out; Gemini
  is told never to estimate one. The first real answer (Recipe: Frost Oil) gave Bro'kin in Alterac
  Mountains, 38.2, 38.9, from metabot.gg, fandom.com and foreveroth.com: Classic-era pages, so the
  in-game answer names its sources. Claude stays the exact option.
- **Cost and limits.** Each answer is kept 3 days by question and faction, so a repeat by anyone costs
  nothing (0.4s against 7.8s). 30 fresh answers a player a day; logged as `AiRequest` purpose
  `forever_ask`. Gemini 2.5 Flash search grounding: about 1,500 free a day, then about $35 per 1,000

- **Two bugs found on the way (0.6.20, 0.6.21).** The saved-data reader's regexes searched the rest of
  the file on a miss (57s for 1 MB, the app froze), and its number `?:` was typed double, so
  `t is long` never matched and every question read as asked in 1970 and was skipped silently. A
  question while answering is off now gets an answer saying so.

## Prices by time of day (2026-11-14, app 0.6.25)

Chriso's question of 12 Nov: are prices higher in Aussie evening hours than at US peak? `ui.lua`
answers it in two places from `DB.history` (every full scan kept, 0.6.15, `keepScan`):

- **An item's page** (Tracked, Market): the median cheapest price and listings in each four-hour block
  of the player's clock that has readings, dearest and cheapest marked.
- **The Market tab's panel before anything is picked**: the whole house by block. Each scan's level is
  the median over its items of price / that item's median price across every scan kept; a block is
  the mean of its scans. Usual, not a fixed day, so a joke listing can't move it. With two scans the
  halves are symmetric by construction and the panel says so until six.

Checked on 14 Nov against the real saved file (two scans, 5,221 items in both) through
`tod_test.py` in the scratchpad and Redis's Lua: 96 ms, Thursday 12:00-16:00 1.8% above, 00:00-04:00
1.8% below. The whole-house walk is cached by scan count, so it runs once per new scan.

## Holdings (2026-11-14, app 0.6.26)

Chriso: "what we own in bulk and what we paid for them and how the price has moved, the same kind of
thing you would see if you bought crypto or a stock". A tab of its own, since Tracked is prices
watched, Market the whole house and Ledger the history; this is state.

- **Data** (`main.lua`, `DB.holdings[character]`): bags walked on `BAG_UPDATE_DELAYED`, throttled to
  1.5 s; the bank only while open, as `GetItemCount(id, true)` over the bags for every known id plus
  whatever the bank's own bags hold (bag ids tried under `pcall`, since they differ by client); gold on
  `PLAYER_MONEY`. Account-wide, so characters add up; a bank not opened since the feature went in is
  "not seen yet", not zero.
- **Pricing** (`ui.lua`, `costBasis`, `holdings`): average cost per unit from the ledger (buys at the
  price confirmed, crafts at the reagents' value, crafts with a reagent unpriced not counted); units
  known = min(held, bought + made − sold), sales matched by name since invoices carry no item id.
  The rest is said to be unknown. Worth = cheapest listing × held × 0.95. Rows: held ≥ 5, or any
  with a cost known. A total row on top with gold per character.
- **Checked** with `hold_test.py` (scratchpad) under Redis's Lua on made-up data: two characters,
  a buy, a sale, a vendor buy, a craft with no reagent value; every number as worked by hand.

## Depth and sell-through (2026-11-15, app 0.6.28)

The two numbers a trader needs that the scans and the ledger didn't give (Chriso: "do you think the
addon now tracks all the data you would need to make a lot of gold?").

- **Depth** (`main.lua`, `depthOf`): each scan keeps, per item, the price of the 10th, 50th and 200th
  cheapest unit (`a.d`, and `:p10:p50:p200` after the count in the history string, blank past what's
  listed). Worked out from a units-at-each-price map built during the read and dropped after. Holdings
  values a lot at the deepest tier it reaches, not the one cheapest listing (`sellPriceFor`), and the
  item pages show the ladder (`depthLine`). The history cap went to 6 MB for it.
- **Sell-through** (`main.lua` mail hooks, `ui.lua` `listingStats`): expired and cancelled auctions come
  back as mail ("Auction expired: X"); taking the item writes a ledger line (`expired`, `cancelled`),
  matched by subject pattern built from the game's own `AUCTION_EXPIRED_MAIL_SUBJECT`. Per item:
  posted, sold, back unsold, cancelled, still up, sold share of what's resolved, and the median hours
  from post to sale (each sale against the oldest post still unmatched). On the Holdings row ("80% of
  what you post sells") and every item page.
- **Checked** under Redis's Lua with `main_test.py` and `hold_test.py` (scratchpad): depth tiers,
  a thin item, the subject patterns, and a 300-posted / 200-sold / 50-expired item: 80%, 10 hours,
  worth at the 200th unit's price.

## Traps already hit

- **Never toggle trainer filters while reading a trainer** (`SetTrainerServiceTypeFilter`). Each
  toggle fires `TRAINER_UPDATE`, and the loop froze the game.
- SavedVariables are written only at a /reload or logout. A new file in the addon needs a full game
  restart, not a /reload.
- **Wowhead rate-limits after about 40 fast page loads** (HTTP 405). Fetch slowly, one page every few
