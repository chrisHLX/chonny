----- RESOLVED via clustering analysis, 2026-08-14 -----
User asked for a histogram/consistency pass instead of "max observed" — with 79 matches on
file this was decisive. The earlier "max" methodology was the wrong estimator for these:
a modal (most common) value with a clean, dominant majority is much stronger evidence than
whatever the single highest outlier happened to be — max only tells you an upper bound, mode
tells you what actually usually happens.

  Blind (2094): 37/63 instances at 5.0s (next-largest bucket is scattered noise) -> 5s. Was 6.
  Kidney Shot (408): 75/152 at 5.0s, >3x the next bucket -> 5s. Was 6 (reverses the earlier
    verbal "kidney 6" recollection — 152 real instances across 36 matches is much stronger
    evidence than a single remembered figure).
  Paralysis (115078): 30/55 at 3.0s -> 3s. Was 3.9 (the earlier "max" of 3.9 was just the one
    longest outlier, not representative).
  Blinding Sleet (207167): 71/177 at 4.0s -> 4s. Was 5.2.
  Blinding Sleet (317898): 56/144 at 6.0s -> 6s. Was 7.8. IMPORTANT: this is a completely
    different peak from 207167's 4.0s peak, not the same spell measured twice — these two
    spell_ids are genuinely mechanically distinct, not duplicate copies of one ability. The
    curated list's flat "4s" for "Blinding Sleet" apparently only covered 207167.

All 5 applied and live. Only Strangulate's earlier "modified by dragon ability" caveat is
still open as a real unknown (what the modifier actually is) — its value (4s) is applied and
not in question, just the mechanism behind why one measured instance read higher.
